What counts as one video?

One successfully completed story video uses one video from your plan. Failed or cancelled work does not use your allowance.

What are scene images?

They are the different images made to match important moments in your story. An image may be reused with movement when the same scene continues.

How renewal works

Paid access lasts 30 days. Sleep Studio does not charge you again automatically; you choose when to renew.
