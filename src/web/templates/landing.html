Your finished videos, thumbnails, and uploaded videos use storage. The same video is not counted twice.

Your files stay safe. Delete an old video or choose a bigger plan before making another one.

Common questions

Know what you get before you start.

What does My Automation Studio create?

It turns a story idea into a finished video with a script, matching images, narration, captions, sound, a thumbnail, and publishing details.

Do I need video editing experience?

No. Choose your idea, length, voice, and picture style. We assemble the video for you to review.

Can I publish videos to YouTube?

Yes. Connect your YouTube channel and choose Public, Unlisted, or Private. Download the video for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

Can I use it for free?

Yes. You can create an account, explore the workspace, and prepare your Video Recipe. Choose a paid plan when you want to create a video.
