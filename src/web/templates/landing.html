What counts as one video?

One successfully completed story video uses one video from your plan. A failed or cancelled creation does not use your allowance.

What does the image limit mean?

It is the number of different scene images created for one video. Images may be reused with movement when the story continues in the same place.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. A paid plan lasts 30 days. You choose when to renew through Paystack.
