# Bedtime Stories Channel — Complete Launch TODO

Use this checklist from the first decision through launch, optimisation and scaling.

## Definition of done

The channel is considered successfully launched when:

- [ ] Its audience and bedtime-story niche are clearly defined.
- [ ] Branding and YouTube settings are complete.
- [ ] My Automation Studio is configured for the channel.
- [ ] Ten reviewed videos have been published.
- [ ] At least eight weeks of consistent publishing have been completed.
- [ ] Analytics have been reviewed and the best-performing format is known.
- [ ] A repeatable monthly production calendar is running.

---

## Phase 1 — Choose the exact audience

Do not mix children’s bedtime stories and adult sleep stories on one channel.

- [ ] Choose one primary audience:
  - [ ] **Adult sleep stories — recommended:** calm fictional journeys for adults who want to relax or fall asleep.
  - [ ] **Children’s bedtime stories:** simple stories made primarily for children, with the correct “Made for Kids” setting.
- [ ] Write the final audience choice here: `____________________________`
- [ ] Confirm that every future title, thumbnail, narrator and story will serve this audience.

### Recommended starting niche

**Cozy sleep stories for adults:** peaceful journeys, quiet hotels, rainy towns, libraries, trains, forests, cottages and gentle mysteries without danger.

Why this is recommended:

- Long videos can produce useful watch time.
- Calm fictional stories fit the existing automation.
- There is no historical fact-checking burden.
- The same visual and sound style can become recognisable.
- The channel can later expand into longer stories and compilations.

## Phase 7 — Establish the story rules

Every adult sleep story should:

- [ ] Open gently without a loud hook.
- [ ] Introduce one clear traveller, resident or guide.
- [ ] Use simple names that are easy to pronounce.
- [ ] Establish a safe destination or purpose.
- [ ] Move through calm, visually distinct scenes.
- [ ] Avoid villains, arguments, chases and sudden danger.
- [ ] Use sensory details such as rain, warm light, wind, water or a fireplace.
- [ ] Keep sentences easy to follow with closed eyes.
- [ ] Slow down during the final third.
- [ ] End safely and completely.
- [ ] Avoid repeated characters, names and locations across videos.

### Standard automation instruction

```text
Write a gentle bedtime story for adults using simple, soothing language and an easy-to-follow plot. Begin calmly, introduce a safe journey or quiet task, and move through several peaceful locations. Avoid danger, arguments, shouting, horror, difficult grammar and sudden events. Use sensory details such as soft rain, warm light, wind, water and distant natural sounds. Give every meaningful story scene a matching visual. Gradually slow the pace and end with comfort, safety and rest.
```

## Phase 9 — Review every generated video

- [ ] Watch the entire video before upload.
- [ ] Confirm that the narrator remains consistent.
- [ ] Confirm that narration matches the captions.
- [ ] Remove captions that cover important visual details.
- [ ] Check that the main character remains visually consistent.
- [ ] Check that every image matches its story scene.
- [ ] Remove frightening, distorted or threatening images.
- [ ] Ensure movement is slow and smooth.
- [ ] Ensure transitions are not bright or sudden.
- [ ] Ensure music and ambience remain below the narrator.
- [ ] Remove sudden footsteps, impacts or loud sound effects.
- [ ] Check that the thumbnail is bright enough to understand on a phone.
- [ ] Confirm that thumbnail text uses no more than four words.

## Phase 13 — Track results after every upload

Review each video after 48 hours and again after seven days.

- [ ] Record impressions.
- [ ] Record click-through rate.
- [ ] Record views.
- [ ] Record average view duration.
- [ ] Record average percentage viewed.
- [ ] Check retention during the opening 30 seconds.
- [ ] Record subscribers gained.
- [ ] Read comments for requests and complaints.
- [ ] Note which thumbnails receive the most clicks.
- [ ] Note which locations, narrators and sound styles hold attention longest.

Do not judge the channel from one upload. Review patterns after at least ten videos.

---

## Phase 14 — Improve after the first 10 videos

- [ ] Rank all videos by watch time.
- [ ] Rank all videos by click-through rate.
- [ ] Identify the best two settings or story types.
- [ ] Produce three related stories for each winning type.
- [ ] Replace weak thumbnails where impressions are high but clicks are low.
- [ ] Improve openings where viewers leave early.
- [ ] Reduce or remove sounds mentioned negatively in comments.
- [ ] Keep the best-performing narrator consistent.
- [ ] Stop repeating locations or titles that consistently underperform.

---

## Phase 15 — Expand video length carefully

- [ ] Start with 8–10-minute stories.
- [ ] After five successful videos, test one 15–20-minute story.
- [ ] Compare its watch time and production cost with shorter videos.
- [ ] Only move toward 30–60 minutes when retention and generation reliability support it.
- [ ] Create long compilations from stories already owned by the channel.
- [ ] Add clean transitions between compilation stories.
- [ ] Do not loop a five-second video visibly for an hour.
