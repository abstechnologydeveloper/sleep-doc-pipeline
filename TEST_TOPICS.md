# Storytelling Pipeline Test Topics

Use these original concepts to test scripts, narration, images, captions, thumbnails, transitions, and final videos. The language should stay simple, warm, and fun. Sleep stories may have mystery or gentle suspense, but should avoid graphic violence, loud shocks, and copyrighted characters.

## Validation checklist

- The narration uses simple words and sounds natural when spoken.
- The opening creates curiosity without making a false promise.
- The story remains suitable for its listed audience.
- The thumbnail is colorful, clear, truthful, and readable at a small size.
- Speech is audible and the music or sound effects do not cover the voice.
- Captions use short phrases and remain close to the spoken words.
- Images, transitions, and movement fit the current part of the story.
- The final MP4 contains visible video and audible audio.
- A failed run keeps reusable files and can resume without starting over.
